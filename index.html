Each <part> (or each staff of a single multi-staff part) is drawn on its own staff — the first part encountered goes on top, the next on the bottom — positioned by that staff's own current clef, independent of the other staff's pitch range. The clef and key signature at the left edge always reflect whatever is active at the current scroll position; a smaller marker also appears at the exact point further along where a clef or key change actually happens. The vertical gap between the two staves defaults to 5 staff-spaces and automatically grows for whatever's currently scrolled into view, if ledger lines from the two staves would otherwise collide. Note accidentals prefer an explicit <accidental> tag (so natural is shown correctly, and parentheses="yes" is honored); otherwise they fall back to the note's <alter> value. Notes are positioned by real musical time (via divisions, backup, forward), so spacing is duration-accurate — the foundation the counterpoint-analysis and note-editing stages will build on next. Ties (<notations><tied type="start"/></notations> paired with a matching type="stop" of the same pitch) are drawn as a curved line between the two noteheads. The OVHI checkbox (outside-voices harmonic intervals) adds interval labels — e.g. P5, M3, m6, PU for a perfect unison — below the bottom staff, one per point where either voice's actual pitch changes (including oblique motion, where one voice sustains while the other moves, and repeated identical intervals like parallel fifths, which each get their own label since the notes themselves changed); repeating the exact same two pitches doesn't add a new label. Compound intervals are reduced to their simple form within an octave. Rhythm symbols (stems, beams, flags) aren't drawn yet, just noteheads and accidentals. Only files with two staves total are supported for now.

Uploading a score

Upload a two-staff score to render it on independent staves: MusicXML (.xml / .musicxml / compressed .mxl) or a native MuseScore 3/4 file (.mscz / .mscx).

Voice numbers always count from 1 on each staff (voice 1 = the first voice of that staff), even though some programs — MuseScore included — number the second staff's voices 5–8 inside the file.

If importing a MusicXML file originally exported from NoteFlight, make sure to leave the first measure blank (make sure your music starts in the second measure). There is currently (2026) a bug in the way NoteFlight exports MusicXML files.
